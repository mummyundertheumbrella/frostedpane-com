Resources for indie artists. These tools and services are available to you in supporting your art. Each comes with a brief description, but if you have questions about any of them, feel free to reach out. If you've used them and have specific suggestions or complaints about them, let us know.

Resources

Bowker

Bowker — ISBN registration & book metadata, U.S.

The U.S. ISBN agency, and the source record for a title's core metadata — publisher, format, and publication date all originate here before they propagate out to retailers and libraries.

Scrivener

Literature & Latte — writing & outlining software, Mac/Windows

Corkboard and outline views make it easy to draft out of order and rearrange scenes later, then export clean into Word or straight into formatting.

Vellum

Vellum Software — ebook & print formatting, macOS only

Turns a manuscript into a clean, professional ebook and print file without touching code. Mac only, so see Atticus below if you're on Windows.

Recommended by Alex Thane

Atticus

Atticus — ebook & print formatting, browser-based (Windows/Mac/Linux)

The closest cross-platform equivalent to Vellum. Runs in the browser, so there's nothing to install, and works the same regardless of your operating system.
